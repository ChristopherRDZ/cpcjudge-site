{# The tournament bracket, shared by the projector screen and the organizer page. Every match is placed by calculation, exactly halfway between the two matches it comes from, and joined to them by SVG lines. The whole thing sits in a viewport that is dragged to move around and zoomed with the wheel, a pinch or the buttons. A bracket too big for the window is not shrunk until nobody can read it: it opens at its normal size on what is being played, and "Show all" shows it whole for whoever wants the overview. Plain script and inline CSS, nothing under resources/ and no compress block. #}
